Settings

RiftScout gets your profile, match history and lobby scouting straight from the League client. A key only adds searching players on other servers and collecting extra build data on this PC.

Keys come from developer.riotgames.com. Development keys expire every 24 hours. The key stays on this computer – encrypted with your Windows account when Windows allows it – and is never shown in RiftScout's activity list or error messages.
